
# About Callijatra[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span)

**Callijatra FOUNDATION**[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span)

Callijatra Foundation is a youth-led initiative dedicated to the revival of the Ranjana script, Nepal Lipi, and the Nepalbhasa language, with a mission to preserve Nepal's rich cultural heritage.[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span) By blending traditional practices with modern digital innovation, Callijatra bridges the past and the present — ensuring that ancient scripts, languages, and artistic traditions remain accessible and relevant in today's digital age.[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)

The initiative brings together a community of artists, calligraphers, designers, developers, teachers, and cultural enthusiasts, fostering learning through workshops, creative collaborations, calligraphy challenges, educational content, mobile applications, fonts, and public exhibitions.[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)

> *The word "Jatra" means festival in Nepali — Callijatra is a festival of calligraphy.*[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)

Founded in 2017 as a creative challenge inviting participants to submit calligraphy in any script on a given theme, Callijatra has grown into a national movement — through Lipi mobile apps, script-based digital fonts, calligraphy tools, video tutorials and online courses, books and learning materials, and workshops, live calligraphy, exhibitions and cultural events.[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)

---

### This keyboard[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)

Nepal Lipi Keyboard is one of those Lipi tools — a way to type Ranjana script, Nepal Lipi and Nepalbhasa on an ordinary phone, with layouts, dictionaries and transliteration for scripts that most keyboards have never supported.[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span) Writing a script every day is what keeps it alive; this is meant to make that ordinary.[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)

### Mission[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)

To preserve, promote, and revitalize Nepal's indigenous scripts and the Nepalbhasa language, so that these traditions remain:[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)

* relevant in modern society[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)
* accessible through digital tools and learning resources[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)
* actively practiced, taught, and celebrated[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)
* sustainable for future generations[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)

By supporting artisans, calligraphers, and educators with platforms and opportunities, the Foundation also contributes to sustainable livelihoods within cultural and creative industries.[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span)

### How the work happens[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)

* Hands-on workshops on Ranjana script, Nepal Lipi and traditional writing systems, and training for youth, students and educators to learn and teach them[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)
* Lipi mobile apps, script fonts, calligraphy tools and practice materials for self-paced learning[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span)
* Calligraphy challenges, exhibitions, festivals and public demonstrations, giving local artisans a place to teach, earn and show their work[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span)
* Books, practice guides and educational texts, and the documenting of traditional scripts in print and digitally[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span)
* Partnerships with schools, cultural institutions and libraries, and mentorship between script experts and new learners[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span)
